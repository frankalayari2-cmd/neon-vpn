Privacy Policy for Neon VPN

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Overview & Zero-Log Commitment

Neon VPN ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your online privacy and security. We operate under a strict No-Logs Policy. We do not monitor, record, log, or store your browsing history, traffic destination, DNS queries, or originating IP address.

2. Use of Android VpnService

Neon VPN utilizes the official Android VpnService API solely to establish a secure, encrypted device-level tunnel (using V2Ray/Xray protocols) between your device and our cloud proxy nodes. This encrypted tunnel protects your data on public Wi-Fi networks and prevents third-party interception. All traffic routed through VpnService is encrypted and never inspected or stored on our servers.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To keep Neon VPN free for users worldwide, we display optional rewarded sponsor advertisements inside an in-app browser view. Our advertising partners may use standard non-personal device identifiers or cookies to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud. Users can choose when to view a sponsor page to extend their free session time.

4. Data Storage on Your Device

Basic app preferences—such as your remaining free session timer and cached server node configurations—are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and are never transmitted to external databases.

5. Children's Privacy

Neon VPN is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
